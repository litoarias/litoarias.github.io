# App Pages, Legal And Store Links

- **WEB-APP-001 (MUST)** Every app landing has privacy, support, and (when the
  app has purchases or accounts) terms pages, in every shipped language.
- **WEB-APP-002 (MUST)** Legal pages refer to "the Developer"; no personal name,
  email, address, or phone (CORE-PRIV-001/002). Contact goes through an
  anonymous form.
- **WEB-APP-003 (MUST)** The privacy policy matches what the shipped app does
  (analytics, ads, AI processing, cloud sync, health data). Update it in the
  same change as the app when data collection changes (CORE-PRIV-004).
- **WEB-APP-004 (MUST)** URLs registered in App Store Connect (privacy policy,
  support, marketing) never break. Check them before restructuring.
- **WEB-APP-005 (MUST)** Apps with AdMob keep a valid `app-ads.txt` at the
  root of the developer website domain.
- **WEB-APP-006 (MUST)** Store badges and links use the official artwork and
  the correct App Store ID per market.
